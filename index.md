# Big File Cleaner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Big File Cleaner runs entirely on your PC.

- **No data leaves your computer.** The app does not send file names, file contents, scan results or any other information anywhere.
- **No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.**
- **Passwords:** the Admin and View passwords you choose are stored only on your PC, as salted one-way hashes. The passwords themselves are never stored.
- **Saved scans and logs:** lists of large files and deletion logs are stored only on your PC, in the app's own data folder. Resetting or uninstalling the app removes them.
- **Purchases:** buying Big File Cleaner Pro is handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement. The app only asks the Store whether Pro has been purchased.
